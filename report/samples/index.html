Prior art (honest)

Pieces of this exist, but not this loop as a packaged tool. kube-hunter and peirates probe or attack a cluster and stop at findings. Kubescape and Polaris scan posture and do not fire a live admission battery. Kyverno Chainsaw tests policies you already wrote; Policy Reporter visualizes results. The closest cousins are IronClaw `--emit-policy` and Valqore's generate_policy, which emit Kyverno/Gatekeeper YAML from static scan findings, and blog-level K8sGPT pipelines that draft policies and dry-run them with the Kyverno CLI. None of those run the same attack manifests against Kyverno, Gatekeeper, and built-in PSA, then auto-generate a policy for what was admitted and re-run the attack on the live webhook to prove the gap closed. That closed loop is the novel part of this lab. This stage adds a live Kali-tool front end (nmap, nuclei, peirates, trivy) that feeds those findings into the same loop.
